INTERPRETATION

Different endpoints answer different questions

From a molecular hypothesis to its test

GGTA1 targeting and human complement-regulator expression motivate complementary measurements: antibody recognition, complement deposition and serum-mediated injury. Template-based structural summaries can help choose follow-up sites; they do not measure binding affinity or signaling. The resource connects these hypotheses with sources and assay choices, informing experimental evaluation of clinically relevant donor designs.

Independent candidate-specific assays, matched individual and combined intervention arms, and clinical-outcome studies remain necessary to establish antigenicity, comparative edit benefit, synergy and donor-selection performance. Original evidence labels and score weights are retained.
